# iOS third-party notices

The iOS target resolves these binary Swift packages at build time:

- `sbooth/opus-binary-xcframework` 0.3.0 — Opus, Opusfile and libopusenc XCFramework; Opus license in the upstream package.
- `sbooth/ogg-binary-xcframework` 0.1.3 — Ogg XCFramework; BSD-3-Clause license in the upstream package.

Redistributions must retain the copyright notices, conditions and disclaimers shipped by both packages. These dependencies are unrelated to the separately licensed proprietary MoDi Protocol binaries under `third_party/modi-protocol/`.
